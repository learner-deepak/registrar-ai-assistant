Registrar Office Official Notice
Azim Premji University, Bengaluru
Subject: Academic Guidelines and Degree Exit Policy

For students pursuing the dual degree programs or the Bachelor of Arts Honors in Economics, the standard duration of the honors track is 4 years. 

However, students are permitted to opt for a standard bachelor's degree exit after completing 3 years of study, provided all foundational credit requirements are met by the end of the 6th semester. 

For further details regarding internship requirements (June-July window), please consult the career services portal.
